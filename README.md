# Fair Notes

Quick notes for each company at the MSU Fall 2026 Career Fair (Sep 30). The company list is preloaded from my mechanical engineering Summer 2027 internship game plan.

**Live:** https://samjayf26-prog.github.io/fairnotes/

- Tap a company, then **I talked to them**. The notes box opens with the cursor already in it.
- Rate interest from 1 to 5, add quick tags, the rep's name and contact, how to apply, and the deadline.
- The **Follow-up** tab has a checklist for each company (apply, thank-you email, LinkedIn, follow up), sorted by deadline.
- The **Review** tab ranks your notes by interest. You can copy them all as text or download a backup.

Notes are saved on your device (localStorage) with no account. Plain HTML/CSS/JS with no build step.
